AoESolo - AI danh AoE 1 theo luat D3KT

1. AoESolo_full.zip: kem san game trong thu muc Games, launcher tu dung game do.
   AoESolo.zip: KHONG kem game, can co san AoE 1 Rise of Rome ban AoE-HD tren may (vi du ban cai qua XArena).
2. Giai nen o dau cung duoc (ra thu muc AoESolo), chay AoESolo.exe trong thu muc do.
3. Launcher tu tim thu muc game (registry cua bo cai, C:\Program Files\XArena\Game\AoE-HD, cac o dia...).
   Khong thay thi bam "Chon..." va tro toi thu muc co EMPIRESX.EXE.
4. Neu thu muc game nam trong Program Files, launcher hoi de chep ban game tren may sang
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\AoESolo\AoE-HD va dung ban chep do.
5. Nhap ten, chon so may va phe, bam "Choi". Nen choi toc do x2; may yeu nen de 1-4 bot.

Ma nguon va mo ta: https://github.com/trandinhnamuet/aoe-bot-d3kt

6. Launcher tu kiem tra ban moi khi mo; co ban moi thi bam nut "Cap nhat" o thanh duoi, no tu tai, thay file va mo lai.
7. Launcher gui thong ke su dung an danh (ma may ngau nhien, phien ban, so bot moi tran) de biet co bao nhieu nguoi dung.
   Khong gui noi dung tran hay file nao. Tat bang o "Gui thong ke su dung" trong Cai dat tran.
8. Gap loi: bam "Gui log cho dev" o thanh duoi de gui nhat ky phien choi cho nguoi phat trien (kem thong tin CPU/RAM
   cua may). Sau moi tran launcher cung tu gui (tat bang o "Gui thong ke su dung").
